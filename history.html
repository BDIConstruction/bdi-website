Our Story

Built on a foundation that goes back further than our name.

Teobaldo Rosell Jr. founded BDI Construction Company in 1994 — with experience in construction dating back to 1974.

Today, BDI is a second-generation, family-owned general contracting, design-build, and construction management firm, led by Teobaldo Rosell, III and Carlos Rosell, and serving clients statewide across Florida.

More than three decades later, the standard set at our founding is still the one we hold every project to.

The people behind the name

Founder

Teobaldo Rosell Jr.

Founded BDI Construction Company in 1994, bringing experience in construction dating back to 1974.

Earned a Bachelor of Industrial Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology in 1966, and a Business Administration graduate degree from the University of Miami in 1967.

President

Teobaldo Rosell, III

Provides executive oversight of corporate strategy, accounting, and finance, ensuring the company's financial strength as BDI continues to grow.

Earned a Bachelor of Business Administration in 1990 and a Master of Business Administration with a specialization in Accounting in 1992, both from the University of Miami.

Vice President / Director of Operations

Carlos Rosell

Oversees BDI's day-to-day construction operations — including project management, scheduling, and resource allocation — ensuring every project is delivered safely, on schedule, and to BDI's standards for quality.

A Certified General Contractor in the State of Florida. Earned a Bachelor of Business Administration in 1992 and a Master of Business Administration with a specialization in Marketing and International Business in 1993, both from the University of Miami.

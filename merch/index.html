How it works

  • Office can order up to 5 shirts and Construction up to 8. BDI pays half of every shirt. The price shown is your half.
  • Everyone also gets 1 free hard hat and 1 free safety vest if you need them.
  • BDI places one bulk order for everyone and adds the new logo. The logo, tax and shipping are on BDI.
  • Your half is deducted from your paycheck. Check the authorization box before you send your order.
  • Hard hats and vests are paid 100% by BDI and don't count toward your shirt limit.
  • Only the approved styles and colors get the logo, so everyone's gear matches.

History  /  Culture

From charity events to career fairs, we show up for the communities we build in — not just the buildings themselves.

At BDI, we reinforce our company culture and philosophy through company events and celebrations held throughout the year. We always give back, through sponsorships and through donations to a range of organizations — and through the topping out celebrations that bring everyone who has worked on a project together to mark it reaching its full height.

We believe our continued success is directly attributable to the pride our team takes in the performance of their work and the character of our firm. We support our clients, and we support our community.

Pre-Construction

Where the budget and the schedule are decided

Most of what a project will cost, and most of what can still be changed about it, is settled before anyone breaks ground. Pre-construction is that work.

Lourdes Escandon

Director of Pre-Construction

An architect by training, Lourdes brings more than fifteen years leading pre-construction for multifamily, mixed-use, educational, commercial and public-sector work, on projects from several million dollars to over $100 million. Four of those years have been at BDI, and they are the four the company has spent growing — her department has had to scale with it.

She leads a team of four: a senior estimator, junior estimators and an assistant. Between them they own budgeting, estimating, value engineering, constructability review, procurement, scheduling and risk management — working alongside owners, developers, architects and consultants to establish realistic budgets, test whether a project is feasible, and line its goals up against cost, schedule and quality.

The result is what a client most needs early on: cost certainty while the decisions are still cheap to make, and less risk carried into the ones that follow.

Construction

Three divisions, three directors

Construction at BDI is split three ways: two divisions by region, and one by the kind of project. Each has a director who owns delivery inside it — the schedule, the budget, the safety of every site, the quality of the work, and the teams doing it.

Yamile Dominguez

Director of Construction
South Florida

A civil engineer by training, Yamile has been with BDI for twenty-five years — longer than the divisions she now works alongside, which took their present shape as the company grew — and she has had a hand in most of what BDI has built in that time. She is one of the pillars the place is built on.

She leads all construction in the South Florida division, BDI's larger building work, overseeing two project managers and the projects under them while carrying one of her own.

Her remit runs from schedules and production flow across every job in the division to design review, budget and execution on each one. She holds every site to the company's safety policies and to OSHA and EPA requirements, drives the quality programme, and oversees warranty work after handover — and she builds the working relationships with subcontractors and suppliers that the whole division depends on.

Scott Lowrance

Director of Construction
Central & West Florida

Scott leads all construction in Central and West Florida — the largest projects BDI builds, and the largest field team behind them. A project manager and an assistant project manager report to him, and like Yamile he runs a project of his own.

He holds a thorough command of the construction contract, the specifications, and the civil and architectural plans on every job, which is what allows a division of that size to be delivered on time, on budget and to standard. Safety and OSHA and EPA compliance, quality, production processes and warranty all sit with him.

Christopher Alvarez

Director of Construction
Special Projects

Christopher brings more than twenty years of construction leadership across educational, institutional, commercial and renovation work in South Florida. He runs BDI's special projects division — the small-cap end of the business, roughly $100,000 to a couple of million: interior renovations and build-outs, commercial fit-outs, and work on college and university campuses.

These are the jobs where schedule and logistics matter most, because the building rarely empties out for the work — a treatment suite, a classroom block or an office floor has to keep running around it. He leads project teams through every phase, with oversight of scheduling, logistics, quality control, safety and delivery, working closely with owners, architects, consultants and subcontractors to head off problems rather than react to them. It is what has earned him the trust of clients across the educational and institutional sectors.

Public Programs

Two divisions built around public clients

Public work is bought differently from private work — different contracts, different agencies, different rules about who may be on site and when. BDI runs two divisions dedicated to it, each led by a project manager who owns the division's projects and the team on them.

David Galdeano

Project Manager
United States Postal Service

David has been with BDI for ten years and runs the division's postal work, with a superintendent and an assistant superintendent under him. He plans every phase of the construction life cycle from initiation to completion, and holds the budget, the timeline and the quality control plan for each project against it.

Day to day that means coordinating architects, engineers and trade specialists, keeping clients informed with progress reports, obtaining permits and approvals, and checking every job against local, state and national building codes and safety requirements. Testing and inspection logs, quality assurance reports and punch lists are his, and he runs the schedule and project record in Procore.

His projects include the new Tamiami USPS facility, at $3.4 million; upgrades and renovations to an FDOT vehicle maintenance facility in North Dade, at $2.8 million; and the Amerant Bank branch on Coral Way.

Lazaro Villar

Project Manager
Government

Lazaro runs BDI's government division — public schools, municipal buildings and other publicly funded work — with a team of six behind him.

He came to BDI after most of a twenty-eight-year career in the same work: school district, college and municipal projects across South Florida, much of it delivered CM at Risk, and much of it phased around campuses and buildings that stayed open throughout. He has run a satellite office, supervised project managers and field staff, and negotiated contracts with agencies, owners' representatives and architects — which is most of what a public client is actually buying. He works in Primavera and Procore, and knows the SREF standards Florida school construction is held to.

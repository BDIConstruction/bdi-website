Leadership Team

Accounting

Every project, accounted for

Construction runs on money moving correctly and on time — out to subcontractors, in from owners, against a budget that has to hold from award to closeout.

Andrea Loguzzo

Controller

Andrea has been with BDI for sixteen years. She joined in accounts receivable and is now the company's Controller, with a background in business administration.

She oversees BDI's accounting operations — the controls and procedures money moves through, billing to owners, collections, financial statements and reporting. She works alongside the project teams to track each job against its budget, and makes sure the subcontractors who build BDI's work are paid properly and on time.

Pre-Construction

Where the budget and the schedule are decided

Most of what a project will cost, and most of what can still be changed about it, is settled before anyone breaks ground. Pre-construction is that work.

Lourdes Escandon

Director of Pre-Construction

An architect by training, Lourdes brings more than fifteen years leading pre-construction for multifamily, mixed-use, educational, commercial and public-sector work, on projects from several million dollars to over $100 million. She joined BDI four years ago and has built the department out alongside the company's own growth.

She leads a team of estimators. Between them they own budgeting, estimating, value engineering, constructability review, procurement, scheduling and risk management, working alongside owners, developers, architects and consultants to establish realistic budgets and test what a project can carry.

The work gives an owner what they need before a shovel is in the ground: a number reliable enough to underwrite against, a schedule that holds up to sequencing, and the scope gaps found while they can still be designed out rather than priced back in as change orders.

Construction

Four divisions, four directors

BDI works through separate departments, each with its own kinds of project, its own schedules and its own budgets to answer for. Four directors carry construction between them, one to a division, and each owns delivery inside it.

Yamile Dominguez

Director of Construction
South Florida

A civil engineer by training, Yamile has been with BDI for twenty-five years and has had a hand in most of what the company has built in that time. She is one of the pillars the place is built on.

She leads construction across South Florida on projects of every size, overseeing project managers and the work under them while carrying a project of her own.

Schedules, budgets, design review and the flow of work across every job in the division sit with her, along with the quality of what gets built and the safety of the people building it. She holds the relationships with subcontractors and suppliers the division runs on, and stays with a project through warranty once it is handed over.

Scott Lowrance

Director of Construction
Central & West Florida

Scott leads construction across Central and West Florida, where BDI builds some of its largest projects. He runs a team of project managers and field staff, and carries a project of his own alongside them.

He holds a thorough command of the contract, the specifications and the drawings on every job in the division, which is what allows work at that scale to land on time, on budget and to standard. Schedules, budgets, quality and the safety of every site are his, through to warranty after handover.

Christopher Alvarez

Director of Construction
Special Projects

A Certified General Contractor in the State of Florida, Christopher brings more than twenty years of construction leadership across educational, institutional, commercial and renovation work in South Florida. He leads BDI's special projects division, which takes on projects under $5 million: interior renovations, build-outs and commercial fit-outs, and work on college and university campuses.

It is work that happens around people, because the building rarely closes for it. He leads his teams through every phase, from scheduling and logistics through quality and delivery, working closely with owners, architects and subcontractors.

Lazaro Villar

Director of Construction
Government

Lazaro leads BDI's government division — public schools, municipal buildings and other publicly funded work — with a team of project managers and field staff behind him. He came to BDI after most of a twenty-eight-year career in the same work across South Florida.

Much of that has been delivered CM at Risk, and much of it phased around campuses and buildings that stayed open throughout. He has run a satellite office, supervised project managers and field staff, and negotiated contracts with agencies, owners' representatives and architects, which is most of what a public client is buying.
